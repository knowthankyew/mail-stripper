## 1. Local Redaction Notice
MailStripper is a local-first email sanitization and tracking pixel stripper. It operates locally to neutralize trackers and strip sensitive attributes from correspondence before viewing or forwarding.

## 2. Consumer Privacy Defaults & Air-Gap Invariant
By default, all redactions execute locally. Raw emails and payloads are never transmitted to external servers.

## 3. Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)
A complete, machine-readable CycloneDX Software Bill of Materials (`bom.json`) is generated on build in CI via OWASP `@cyclonedx/cdxgen` and attached to release artifacts.
